    Keyboard shortcuts
    
    
    
      
    
        Toggle marker
        
    Toggle a marker at the live playback position, or the saved position when the video is closed. A marker within two seconds is removed instead. Removing a marker also deletes its annotation. Holding the key does not toggle repeatedly.
    
      
    
      
    
        Show/hide markers
        
    Show or hide the marker lines over the timeline. The preference is saved locally. Hiding markers keeps their data and annotations, including in JSON exports.
    
      
    
      
    
        Add/edit annotation
        
    Add or edit the current marker’s annotation. Without a current marker, use the video-start annotation at 0:00. Chapter titles are the default annotations until you edit or clear them.
    
      
    
      
    
        Save annotation
        
    Save the open annotation editor. Use Ctrl+Enter on Windows/Linux or Command+Enter on macOS. Ordinary Enter adds a new line while editing.
    
      
    
      
    
        Previous/next marker
        
    Move to the previous or next marker. With no further marker, move to the beginning or end of the video without wrapping. Near a selected marker, step through adjacent points, including overlapping markers. An open video seeks without changing playback state; a closed video updates its saved position.
    
      
    
      
    
        Scrub
        
    
      
    
      
    
        Larger scrub
        
    
      
    
      
    
        Previous/next frame
        
    Step one frame backward or forward in an open video. A playing video pauses automatically and stays paused. Frame size comes from the player’s reported frame rate.
    
      
    
      
    
        Playback speed
        
    Decrease or increase playback speed in an open video. These are Shift+comma and Shift+period on common keyboard layouts.
    
      
    
      
    
        Play/pause
        
    Toggle playback for the selected video when it is open. If it is closed, open it at its saved position.
    
      
    
      
    
        Delete bookmark
        
    Apply the selected deletion mode to a current bookmark. Timed mode moves it to trash; Permanent mode deletes it immediately. On an already trashed row, delete it permanently.
    
      
    
      
    
        Open video
        
    Open or switch to the selected video at its saved position. Existing YouTube tabs are reused when possible.
    
      
    
      
    
        Select video
        
    Move focus between search and the visible video rows. These keys do not seek or change the saved position.
    
      
    
      
    
        Focus search
        
    Reveal and focus search. When already typing in search, a slash is inserted normally.
    
      
    
      
    
        Show/hide thumbnails
        
    Toggle video thumbnails and save the preference. Enabled thumbnails load from YouTube’s image server; bookmarks stay local.
    
      
    
      
    
        Copy transcript
        
    Press Shift+t to copy the selected open video’s transcript in your chosen format. A complete supported transcript is required. The existing clipboard is kept if extraction fails.
    
      
    
      
    
        Copy video URL
        
    Copy the selected video’s clean YouTube URL, including its saved timestamp.
    
      
    
      
    
        Copy video JSON
        
    Copy only the selected current video as JSON, including its markers and annotations. Paste that JSON into another Waypoints popup to merge it.
    
      
    
      
    
        Copy all JSON
        
    Press Shift+c to copy all current bookmarks, including rows hidden by search, with their markers and annotations. Trash and preferences are excluded. Also works in Settings.
    
      
    
      
    
        Toggle settings
        
    Open or close Settings outside text fields. Opening focuses the first group so Up/Down works immediately. Closing returns focus to the gear button. Holding the key does not toggle repeatedly.
    
      
    
      
    
        Toggle help
        
    Open or close this shortcut reference. Up/Down or Tab moves through the rows; Enter or Space expands the focused explanation. Multiple explanations can stay open. Expanding a row does not run its shortcut.
